# Savly: UK company setup and Google Play launch plan

**Research checked:** 12 October 2026. Complete the company and payment setup before opening Savly's Google Play organisation account. Recheck fees and platform rules when you apply.

In the UK, the usual equivalent for this plan is a **private limited company (`Ltd`)**, rather than a US `LLC`. A director does not have to live in the UK, but the company needs a compliant UK registered office. Incorporating a company does **not** guarantee approval for a Tide account, a Google Play organisation account, or merchant payouts. [Companies House: directors](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/appoint-directors-and-company-secretaries) · [Companies House: registered office](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/company-address)

## 1. Check that the UK and Tide route works for you

- [ ] **Describe the real business setup before paying to incorporate.** Record where you live, where you will manage and develop Savly, where customers will be, and who will own and direct the company. Ask a UK accountant familiar with your country of residence to check company tax, your personal tax, and any cross-border reporting. A UK virtual address alone does not establish where the business is actually managed. [HMRC guidance on dual-resident companies](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/company-taxation-manual/ctm34530)
- [ ] **Ask Tide to confirm your eligibility for both formation and banking using your actual details.** Tide's [membership guide](https://www.tide.co/help-centre/becoming-a-tide-member/) says a limited-company director may have a home address in the UK **or overseas**, but requires a UK mobile number and a device registered to a UK App Store or Google Play Store. Its [bank-account FAQ](https://www.tide.co/business-current-account/) says an overseas applicant needs a UK residential address, UK phone number, and UK company directorship. These pages conflict about residential address, so obtain Tide's current answer for your country and documents before placing an order. Do not list a virtual office as your home address.
- [ ] **Explain Savly accurately to Tide.** It is software that compares shopping costs and displays FX data; it is not an exchange bureau, payment-transfer service, or holder of customer funds. Tide excludes certain money-service, FX/financial, and “shell company” businesses. Ask whether your actual operating model qualifies, particularly if all management and work take place outside the UK. [Tide's business eligibility rules](https://www.tide.co/help-centre/becoming-a-tide-member/)
- [ ] **Confirm how Tide would deliver the business card and any verification mail** if you live outside the UK. Tide says cards are sent to the registered trading address, while its virtual-office service cannot be used as a trading address. Also confirm that a UK mobile number and UK-region app-store device are workable for you. [Tide membership guide](https://www.tide.co/help-centre/becoming-a-tide-member/) · [Tide virtual-office terms](https://www.tide.co/terms/virtual-office-address-tcs/)

**If Tide declines banking:** stop the Tide bundle route before paying for it. A UK Ltd can be incorporated by another authorised agent or through [Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company), with an independent compliant registered-office provider and a banking provider that accepts your real residency and business operations. Confirm banking and payout eligibility separately; do not assume incorporation resolves them.

## 2. Arrange the addresses and incorporate the Ltd

- [ ] **Choose a legal name, ownership, and activity.** Check that the proposed company name is available, decide the director(s), shareholders and people with significant control (PSC), and choose an accurate SIC activity code. Keep the Savly brand, domain, and company name consistent where practical. Tide's formation application asks for these details. [Tide formation process](https://www.tide.co/company-registration/) · [Companies House registration](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company)
- [ ] **Choose the address service before filing.** Companies House requires a physical, “appropriate” address in the UK jurisdiction where the company is registered; a bare PO Box is insufficient. The registered office is public. A director also needs a public **service address**, while their real **residential address** is supplied privately to Companies House. [Registered-office rules](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/company-address) · [Personal information on the register](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/your-personal-information-on-the-companies-house-register)
- [ ] **Set up a UK virtual director correspondence/service address.** Use this public address for letters to you as a director and, if the provider permits, use the same location for the company's registered office. Tide's Hoxton Mix package currently includes both a London registered office and a director correspondence address; obtain the provider's written terms and confirm that director mail will reach you reliably. If Tide is unavailable, choose an independent provider that offers a compliant director service address. This is a **virtual service address, not a residential address**: give Companies House your actual home address privately, and do not present the virtual address to Tide or Google as proof that you live in the UK. [Companies House: director addresses](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/your-personal-information-on-the-companies-house-register) · [Tide virtual-office terms](https://www.tide.co/terms/virtual-office-address-tcs/)
- [ ] **Review Tide's virtual office as one possible address option.** It currently advertises a London registered office and director correspondence address, through Hoxton Mix, at **£22.99 + VAT monthly or £190 + VAT annually**. This is **not** a residential or trading address. Its terms say that it scans defined official mail, returns other mail, and is linked to the Tide account. Before choosing it, check how mail from Google, Dun & Bradstreet, customers, and other partners would be received; Google/D&B correspondence is not listed among the guaranteed scanned senders. [Tide virtual-office offer](https://www.tide.co/company-registration/virtual-office-address/) · [Tide virtual-office terms](https://www.tide.co/terms/virtual-office-address-tcs/)
- [ ] **Complete identity verification and form the company.** Tide currently advertises company formation for **£14.99** without its optional Company Secretary subscription; the direct Companies House online fee is **£100**. Confirm Tide's checkout price and any recurring add-ons before paying. Directors and PSCs need Companies House identity verification. Save the certificate of incorporation, company number, articles, share documents, and Companies House personal code securely. [Tide formation](https://www.tide.co/company-registration/) · [Companies House fees](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees) · [Identity verification](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verifying-your-identity-for-companies-house)
- [ ] **Keep the address subscription active and monitored.** Tide says that if it incorporates the company but cannot open the account, Hoxton Mix may ask you to change the address; closing Tide cancels the virtual office. If the address changes, update Companies House and HMRC promptly, then Dun & Bradstreet and Google Play. [Tide virtual-office terms](https://www.tide.co/terms/virtual-office-address-tcs/)

## 3. Set up banking, taxes, and business records

- [ ] **Apply for the Tide business current account and complete its checks.** Provide real identity, residence, company, and business-operation evidence. Wait for approval; incorporation and banking are separate decisions. Tide advertises a no-monthly-fee base account, but transfer and foreign-currency charges can apply. [Tide membership guide](https://www.tide.co/help-centre/becoming-a-tide-member/) · [Tide account costs](https://www.tide.co/support/joining/what-is-tide/how-much-does-tide-cost/)
- [ ] **Confirm that the approved account can receive Google Play payouts.** Google's merchant guidance requires a payout bank account in the merchant account's country or region, with account-holder details matching the bank's records; add and verify it in the Google payments profile. For a UK merchant profile, confirm Tide supplies suitable UK account details in the Ltd's legal name. [Google Play merchant-bank requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/7161440?hl=en) · [Bank verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/7161378?hl=en-GB)
- [ ] **Set up the company tax account and filing calendar.** Keep the company's money separate from personal money. Check the HMRC Corporation Tax setup/UTR, tell HMRC when the company becomes active within the applicable three-month period, and schedule annual accounts, Company Tax Returns, and the Companies House confirmation statement. The current online confirmation-statement fee is **£50**. Have the cross-border accountant assess VAT and your personal tax position rather than inferring it from the virtual address. [Company registration and tax](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company) · [Active-company notice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/corporation-tax-trading-and-non-trading) · [Confirmation statements](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/confirmation-statement)
- [ ] **Check the ICO data-protection fee.** Savly handles account email addresses and other user data. The ICO provides a self-assessment to establish whether a fee is due or an exemption applies. [ICO fee checker](https://ico.org.uk/fee-checker)

## 4. Open the Google Play organisation account

- [ ] **Obtain or look up the Ltd's D-U-N-S number.** Google requires one for an organisation developer account and says a new one may take up to 30 days; requesting it from Dun & Bradstreet is free. Ensure the legal name and address agree with Companies House and the intended Google payments profile. [Google's organisation-account requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en-GB10-1)
- [ ] **Prepare the company's public presence.** Set up an official Savly/company website, domain email, working support email, and phone number. Google asks organisation accounts for an organisation website and phone number, plus verified contact and public developer details. The legal name, address, email and phone may be displayed on Google Play. Check whether the chosen virtual office provides documentation Google accepts as evidence of the legal address; this cannot be assumed from Companies House registration alone. [Google account information requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en-GB10-1) · [Google verification guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634888?hl=en)
- [ ] **Register the Play Console organisation account and merchant profile under the Ltd.** Pay Google's current **US$25 one-time developer fee**, complete identity verification, add the UK payout account, and verify it. Keep the company name/address consistent across Companies House, D&B, Google payments, and the bank. [Play Console signup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en-en) · [Merchant account maintenance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634888?hl=en)

## 5. Make Savly ready for a real Play release

- [ ] **Decide the first-release offer and make it real.** Choose launch countries, supported currencies, monthly/annual/lifetime products, prices, and whether cloud history is included. Savly currently uses RevenueCat's **Test Store** in development; release builds reject test billing and do not provide production Premium. Implement real Google Play products, entitlement verification, cancellation/expiry/refund handling, and production FX/authentication before promising Premium. Ensure a purchase can be used as intended without a required Savly login, or explicitly revise that product requirement. [Savly Premium specification](../specs/002-premium-and-history/spec.md) · [Google Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en)
- [ ] **Publish privacy and deletion resources.** Put an accurate privacy policy on the company website and in the app. Because Savly permits account creation, provide an in-app path and a web resource to request deletion of the account and associated data. Complete the Play Data safety form based on the finished app and its SDKs. [Google user-data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en) · [Account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en)
- [ ] **Build a signed, compliant Android App Bundle.** Finalise package name `com.savly.app`, increase the version code for each release, replace the repo's current debug signing for `release`, and protect the upload key. Verify the actual `.aab` targets **Android 16 / API 36 or higher**, the current requirement for new phone apps. EAS Build or a properly configured native Gradle release can produce the bundle; `npm run export:mobile` alone cannot. [Savly Android build configuration](../android/app/build.gradle) · [Google target API policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878?hl=en) · [Expo submission guide](https://docs.expo.dev/submit/android/)
- [ ] **Test the release build on Android devices.** Cover real account verification and deletion, store purchases and renewal/cancellation, FX provenance, offline behavior, camera permission, small screens, and accessibility. Use actual Android screenshots for the store listing; the browser journey screenshots are documentation of the prototype. [Savly release plan](../specs/001-shopping-calculator/plan.md) · [Google listing accuracy guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15191715?hl=en)
- [ ] **Create Savly in Play Console.** Add the listing text, icon, feature graphic, Android screenshots, contact details, privacy URL, countries, content rating, audience, ads/permissions declarations, Data safety answers, and reviewer access to gated features. The listing must describe the released build accurately. [Google app setup guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en&rd=1) · [Review access guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15191715?hl=en)
- [ ] **Use Play test tracks, then submit production.** Start with internal testing, then closed testing and real Google Play purchase tests. Google's 12-testers-for-14-days production-access rule applies to **personal** developer accounts created after 13 November 2023; it is not stated as a condition for a verified organisation account, but closed testing is still advisable. Resolve Play Console errors and submit the production `.aab` for review. [Testing tracks](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en-en) · [Personal-account testing rule](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en-GB10-1) · [Release process](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859348?hl=en-EN)
- [ ] **Monitor after launch.** Watch crashes, purchase entitlement failures, account-deletion requests, API outages, reviews and support mail. Keep UK company filings, address subscription, Tide account, D&B record, and Google payments details current. [Google account maintenance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634888?hl=en) · [UK director responsibilities](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/directors-responsibilities)
